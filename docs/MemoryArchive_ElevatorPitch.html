Premise
25s
Two operators interview The Friend, the model of a witness.
Ashley Wei disappeared from a residential compound four years ago. She never consented to be archived; her record is reconstructed from secondary sources. The Friend — her neighbour, the last person to see her — agreed to be modelled. She remembers consenting. She can revoke it.

The Loop
30s
One turn — four stages.
01 Input — Whisper transcribes audio, a four-band DFT fingerprint tags the speaker, ONNX models read face and hand, E/F line-traces pick up and present objects. 02 Assembly — DialogueComponent builds the system prompt from a graph asset and wraps the user message in Speaker / Holding / Gesture / Emotion brackets. 03 Claude returns structured JSON. 04 Broadcast — ElevenLabs voice, FACS face, PAD update, per-speaker memory, MetaHuman montage.

01:10
New Mechanics
25s
Per-speaker memory, soft doubt, and three branching endings.
Two things are novel. Speaker identification lets the model address each operator distinctly — Speaker A and Speaker B get different registers. And a soft-doubt prompt gates seven memory topics behind which items each speaker has inspected or presented. The climax has three branches — Convergent Disclosure, Divergent Fragmentation, or Recursive Silence — and the model decides which one plays.
