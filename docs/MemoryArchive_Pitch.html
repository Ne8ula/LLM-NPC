0:00 — 0:10 Open point: poster header
This is The Memory Archive. A near-future apparatus built to recover Ashley Wei — a child who disappeared from a Chinese residential compound four years ago. She never consented to be archived.
0:10 — 0:30 Frame point: subject & testimony lines
The only person who agreed to be modeled is The Friend — Ashley’s neighbour, the last person to see her alive. The Friend who lives in this archive is that model. She remembers consenting. She can revoke consent. The piece asks one question: when the model of a witness withdraws consent, what testimony remains?
0:30 — 0:55 The Loop point: section 01 (the four stages)
Two operators speak from outside the diegetic space, visible inside as chrome robotic hands. Voice goes through Whisper, gets tagged by a custom Speaker-ID component, then annotated with whatever object they’re holding, what gesture they’re making, what their face shows. That annotated message goes to Claude — which returns structured JSON: dialogue, emotion update, optional item-give. ElevenLabs v3 speaks it with inline audio tags; a template anim driver picks one of ten MetaHuman face-and-body montages; lip sync rides char-level timestamps.
0:55 — 1:18 The Mechanic point: components grid
Seven inspectable objects sit in The Friend’s back-room store. Hard-gated topics open only when specific items are presented — not just looked at — and only when per-speaker trust crosses a threshold. The conversation can branch three ways: convergent disclosure, divergent fragmentation, or recursive silence — where the model decides the real Friend would not have wanted this continued, and severs the link.
1:18 — 1:30 Close point: tier badge / footer
A speculative-design provocation about consent and witness, on a working multi-modal LLM stack. Tier 2 just shipped. Tier 3 finishes next week.
